
# 🩺 Comprehensive Analysis and Predictive Modeling of UAE Cancer Patient Data

## 📘 Summary

This project presents a comprehensive data analysis and predictive modeling study on cancer patient records from the United Arab Emirates. The dataset, provided by **Internpulse**, includes detailed information on patient demographics, medical history, cancer types, diagnosis dates, outcomes, and lifestyle factors such as smoking.

### 🔍 Objectives
- Identify patterns and trends in cancer diagnoses and outcomes.
- Explore how factors such as age, gender, ethnicity, smoking status, and cancer type influence survival rates.
- Visualize key distributions and recovery trends across various patient segments.
- Build a predictive model using **XGBoost** to classify whether a patient is likely to recover or not.
- Perform **unsupervised clustering (KMeans)** to reveal hidden patient subgroups and risk profiles.

### 📈 Key Findings
- Recovery rates vary significantly by cancer type, with **Skin, Prostate, and Breast cancers** showing the highest survival outcomes.
- **Smoking status** is strongly associated with negative outcomes, highlighting the need for targeted public health interventions.
- The **XGBoost model** achieved a high prediction accuracy (~85%), indicating the feasibility of using machine learning to support clinical decision-making.
- **Clustering analysis** revealed distinct patient profiles based on features like age, weight, comorbidities, and lifestyle, useful for tailored care strategies.

### 💡 Impact
This report not only provides insights into the nature of cancer cases in the UAE but also demonstrates how data science techniques can be applied to enhance healthcare intelligence, inform policy, and improve patient care.
